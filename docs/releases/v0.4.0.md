# oshost v0.4.0

Compared with `v0.3.0` (`0ae5559`). Includes both merged worktrees: SMTP/account recovery (`d0f1971`) and mobile host layouts (`f987d0b`), plus the door testing, configuration visibility and notification changes.

## Email delivery and account recovery

- Owners can configure SMTP during the final onboarding step or under **Settings → Email delivery (SMTP)**. Choose implicit TLS, enforced STARTTLS or a trusted unencrypted relay, configure the sender, and send a test message. Passwords remain encrypted with the installation key and are not returned by the settings API.
- Every account can verify its email address under **Your account**. Recovery by email requires both configured SMTP and a verified address.
- **Forgot password?** supports requesting owner assistance and, when available, sending a reset email. Owners receive a private notification and can generate a reset link for the account from Team settings. Collaborators cannot read these reset-request notifications through history, badges, realtime or push.
- Verification and reset links expire after ten minutes and work once. New links replace earlier links of the same kind. A successful password reset signs out all devices and invalidates outstanding links; ordinary password changes invalidate recovery links too. Public recovery replies do not disclose account eligibility, and email sends have a per-account cooldown.

## Mobile host layouts

- Simplify guest/staff code navigation, calendar controls, search and filters. Compact code cards keep door permissions, breakfast allowance and usage visible, with secondary actions in menus.
- Move doors/access-code JSON transfers into the list heading's overflow menu. Owner-only authorization and append/whole-list replacement safeguards remain in place.
- Give door/code editors more room on phones, hide bottom navigation while editing, and keep Cancel/Save actions accessible. Remove duplicate headings and group optional access-code fields under additional details.
- Align door-list controls, move door deletion under **Manage door**, and show statistics as cards on smaller screens while retaining the desktop table. Improve narrow-screen layouts and Italian/dark-theme presentation.

## Door management and notifications

- Owners can explicitly test opening a door even when it is disabled for guest access. Tests retain confirmation, idempotency and unresolved-request safeguards, and do not consume guest permissions. Disabled doors remain unavailable through guest links, including links opened by logged-in hosts.
- Show the saved device configuration on door detail and edit screens. The new owner-only configuration endpoint returns the existing URL, method, headers, body and response handling without modifying them. General door and guest reads continue to omit credentials.
- Successful door edits generate a notification naming the editor and door, with a link to the door page. Configuration credentials are excluded from the notification.
- Door actions through guest links include the username when the browser has a valid owner or collaborator session. Identity is resolved on the server; expired or invalid host cookies do not block ordinary guest access. All guest-token and grant restrictions still apply.

## Upgrade notes

- Back up the database and runtime keys before upgrading. **Schema version 9** adds encrypted SMTP settings, email verification, temporary account tokens, delivery cooldowns and pending reset requests. Existing accounts are retained and initially have unverified email addresses.
- Configure SMTP and verify addresses to enable email recovery. Owner-assisted recovery remains available without SMTP; a sole owner who loses access without an existing session, another owner or configured verified email recovery needs installation-level recovery.
- Workspace wipe also removes SMTP and recovery records. Portable JSON transfers retain their existing scope; keep a full database backup and encryption keys to restore installation-level settings.
